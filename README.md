# GiftHer

A personalised app you gift your girlfriend. Her photos, your song, a live countdown to your reunion, love notes and milestones. She saves it to her home screen and opens it every morning. Built especially for long distance couples.

**Live site:** https://gifther.surge.sh

## What's here
- `index.html` — the landing page
- `start.html` — the order / onboarding page (form goes to Formspree)
- `img/` — photos used across the site
- `llms.txt`, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml` — SEO and AI crawler files

## Tiers

- **The Bespoke** — from $32.99
